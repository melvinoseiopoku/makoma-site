Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

M’AKOMA is a bracelet for the people you love, so it touches personal things: who they are, and sometimes your voice. This page says exactly what I collect, why, who else handles it, how long it stays, and how you get it deleted. I do not sell your data and I do not use it for advertising.

Who is responsible

M’AKOMA is a limited liability company registered in the United States, run by me, Melvin Osei Opoku. Questions or requests go to oseimelvin@gmail.com, and I read every one.

When you preorder

  • Your answers in the preorder steps: circle or single, how many bracelets, wrist sizes, your email, and how you heard about M’AKOMA. They are attached to your order so I can build and ship the right bracelets.
  • Payment, name, shipping address and phone number are entered on Stripe’s checkout form. Stripe processes the card; I never see or store your full card number. I see the address and phone so I can ship to you and reach you about delivery.
  • A short one-way code made from your IP address, used only to stop abuse of the seat holds. It cannot be turned back into your address.

When you join the waitlist

Your email address (and anything else you choose to type into the form) goes to Buttondown, the service that sends M’AKOMA emails. If you trace your own design onto a bead, the file is stored privately and only linked to your signup. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

The bracelet designer

The names you type when you design a bracelet stay in your browser. They are not sent to me.

Site analytics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights: counts of page views and load times, without cookies. On the preorder page it also counts which step people reach, never what they typed.

The bracelet and the app

  • The names you give your people and your settings stay on your phone.
  • To carry a Pulse or a voice Echo between two bracelets, the M’AKOMA relay stores a device identifier, the display name you choose, and the message itself until it is delivered.
  • Delivered messages are deleted within 24 hours. A message that cannot be delivered is deleted after 7 days.
  • Diagnostic logs stay on your phone unless you choose to send them to me.

Who else handles your data

Only the services that run M’AKOMA, each for its own job: Stripe (payments and order records), Vercel (website hosting, analytics and private file storage), Upstash (holding a seat while you pay), Buttondown (email), Google (a private spreadsheet some site forms write to) and Railway (hosting the relay).

How long it is kept

Order records are kept as long as they are needed to fulfil, refund or support your order and to meet tax and accounting rules. Waitlist details are kept until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them. Relay messages follow the 24-hour and 7-day limits above.

Your choices

Email me to see what I hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. I will reply within 30 days. Records I have to keep by law, such as a completed sale for tax, I keep only for that purpose. Deleting your data does not cancel an order; cancelling an order works as the terms describe.

Changes to this page

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. I will not start using data you already gave me in a new way without asking you first.
