Child safety standards

November 24, 2026

These standards apply to the M’AKOMA app, operated by M’AKOMA Technologies LLC, including private chats, groups, voice messages, photos and videos.

What we prohibit

M’AKOMA prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation. This includes grooming, sexual solicitation or extortion of a child, trafficking, and creating, requesting or sharing child sexual abuse material. These rules apply to anyone under 18 and to real or computer-generated material that sexually exploits or depicts the sexual abuse of a child.

How to report a concern

In the app, press and hold the message, choose Report, and select Sexual content or the reason that best describes the concern. You can also report a person or group from the chat details. Use Block to stop someone from contacting you.

If you cannot use the in-app report, email hello@makoma.io with the subject Child safety report. Include the date, the account or group involved, and a brief description. Please avoid downloading, copying or forwarding suspected abuse material.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States, you can also report suspected online child sexual exploitation directly to the NCMEC CyberTipline.

How we respond

We review child safety reports and restrict accounts or remove material under our control when appropriate. We preserve and report information as required by applicable law. In the United States, we report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children’s CyberTipline when required. We also report to other competent authorities where the law requires it.
