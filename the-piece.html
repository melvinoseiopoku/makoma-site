Questions

When can I get one?

Now. Batch 1 is fully reserved and being built. Batch 2 is open and ships by 8 November. Preorder yours, with a full refund any time before it ships.

How much will it cost?

You see the Batch 2 price on the last step of the preorder, before you pay, with US shipping included. It is fully refundable until it ships.

Do my friends need to buy one too?

No. You buy one bracelet and invite your people to the M’AKOMA app. They can reach you from the app on their phone, and when they get a bracelet of their own, it becomes two-way.

Is it a smartwatch or fitness tracker?

No. M’AKOMA is jewelry first: no screens, no feeds, no step counts, no notifications. Just the few people you choose, with you all the time.
